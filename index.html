How to Play

Controls

  • ↑ or W — Accelerate
  • ↓ or S — Brake / Reverse
  • ← → or A D — Steer
  • Space — Use item
  • Esc — Pause

Items

  • Boost — Short burst of speed.
  • Green Shell — Fires straight ahead.
  • Red Shell — Homes onto the racer in front.
  • Banana — Drops behind; spins out anyone who hits it.
  • Lightning — Shrinks and slows all opponents.
  • Star — Invincibility + extra speed for a few seconds.

Tips

  • Driving off the track slows you way down.
  • Item boxes respawn after a short cooldown.
  • Trailing racers get better items. Rubber-band life!
